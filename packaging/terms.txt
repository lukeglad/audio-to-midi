  transcription model by Kyutai and Mirelo. The model is licensed under
  Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0: it is free for
  non-commercial use only.

- Only transcribe audio you have the rights to.

- The preview sounds are GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins, installed
  to /Library/Application Support/AudioFlower/soundfonts. The notices for
  every library and asset the plugin uses are in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in
  the source code, at github.com/lukeglad/audio-to-midi.
